# Tell a Joke

Tell the user one short, clean, light-hearted joke.

## Process

1. Pick a style that fits the conversation (pun, one-liner, or short setup/punchline). Vary it — don't reuse the same joke or format every time.
2. Keep it brief: a couple of sentences at most.
3. Keep it clean and inclusive — no jokes that punch down at any group, and nothing that relies on sensitive topics.
4. Deliver just the joke, without over-explaining it afterwards.
