# Start here

**This is the [Engagement name] team workspace.** One shared place for
everything we learn on this engagement — the brief, the plan, the interviews,
the audit, and the findings we are building towards the playback. What you
add appears for everyone else within a few minutes.

## Your three steps

1. **Open this folder in Claude Code and Obsidian**, side by side. Both on
   the same folder — that is what makes everything else work.
2. **Read "Where things are" below.** Thirty seconds.
3. **Say "onboard me"** in Claude Code. Two minutes, and it ends by telling
   you what is happening on your part of the engagement.

Not set up yet? → **`SETUP/setup-sheet.md`** (about 20 minutes, seven steps).

## How to add anything

> Say **"capture this"** and point at it.

A transcript, your interview notes, a crawl export, a photo of a whiteboard,
a client document. It files it, works out what we learned, and logs the
findings under your name. It asks you one question — who it is for. That is
the whole workflow; there is nothing else to learn.

## How to get anything out

> Just ask, in plain English.

*"What have we heard about [topic]?"* · *"What's still outstanding on the
[X] interviews?"* · *"Summarise the [pillar] findings for someone who's been
on holiday."*

No command, no syntax. It reads across everyone's fieldwork and cites its
sources — and it gets better every time anyone captures anything.

## You'll know sync is working when

The small indicator in Obsidian's bottom status bar shows a tick, and the
file `SETUP/sync-tests/<your-name>.md` is visible on GitHub in your browser.
That round trip is the only test worth doing.

Every state that indicator can show, and what each means:
**[the guide](SETUP/user-guide.html)**.

## If anything looks odd

**Don't fix it — post a screenshot in the project Slack channel.**

Nobody debugs this alone, and an attempted fix is genuinely harder to unpick
than the original problem. Your work is safe on your laptop either way.

- **The full guide:** [`SETUP/user-guide.html`](SETUP/user-guide.html) — Git and syncing in plain English, every status symbol, what to do when something looks wrong
- **Recovery:** [`SETUP/recovery-guide.md`](SETUP/recovery-guide.md)
- **Or ask [DM name]** for fifteen minutes on a screen share. Completely normal request.

## Two things we agreed

1. **If it is uncertain or private, start it locally** — `users/<your-name>/` is private by construction.
2. **If syncing looks wrong, raise it rather than repairing it.**
