> **General principle:** Green = on track. Amber = there is a problem but we have a plan and are working through it. Red = we have no plan and it needs to be escalated above the team level (e.g. above delivery leadership or to account/CPG level). If everything is green, treat that as a signal to look harder — something is probably being missed.
>
> **Overall** is a 1–2 sentence summary that reflects the other RAG statuses and the headline narrative for the engagement.

| Dimension            | Red                                                                                                       | Amber                                                                                              | Green                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overall              | One or more dimensions are red and require immediate escalation                                           | Issues exist across one or more dimensions, but plans are in place and being worked through        | All dimensions are on track; delivery is proceeding as expected                                               |
| Plan                 | No plan exists, or the plan has broken down to the point it cannot be recovered without escalation        | Plan quality is variable across the team and/or delivery is not on track against the plan          | A clear, shared plan is in place and the team is on track against it                                          |
| RAIDs                | RAIDs are not being tracked and/or delivery is blocked without escalation of one or more RAIDs            | RAIDs are not being tracked consistently, or key RAIDs are actively creating problems for delivery | RAIDs are known, tracked, and being managed effectively                                                       |
| Tech                 | Critical technical issues are blocking delivery or require escalation to resolve                          | Known technical issues are impacting delivery, but a plan to address them is in place              | The tech stack is healthy with no significant issues impacting delivery                                       |
| Team                 | The team is dysfunctional; issues require escalation above the team level                                 | Morale or ways of working need improvement; some dysfunction or friction is present                | Team morale is good and ways of working are effective and appropriate                                         |
| Commercial           | Invoices are not being paid, work is being delivered at risk, or there are unresolved commercial disputes | Issues with invoice payment, or upcoming work that has not yet been commercially signed off        | SOWs are in place for all work being delivered and invoices are being paid on time                            |
| InfoSec & Compliance | Customers or their data are being impacted by a security or compliance issue                              | Known issues exist but none are high severity or causing customer impact                           | An incident response plan is in place, compliance policies are being adhered to and there are no known issues |
